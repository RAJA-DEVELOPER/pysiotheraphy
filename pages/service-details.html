Your Journey

The Treatment Process

A structured, evidence-based rehabilitation pathway tailored to your injury and goals.

1
Days 1–7
Acute Management

Immediately following your injury, our priority is controlling pain and inflammation while protecting damaged tissue. Your therapist will conduct a thorough assessment, apply RICE principles (Rest, Ice, Compression, Elevation), and introduce gentle range-of-motion exercises to prevent stiffness without aggravating the injury.

Techniques Used:
RICE Protocol Pain Management Gentle Mobilisation Electrotherapy (TENS) Initial Assessment
2
Weeks 2–4
Recovery Phase

As initial inflammation subsides, we progressively introduce strength-building exercises, flexibility work, and hands-on manual therapy. Focus shifts to restoring normal movement patterns and addressing contributing biomechanical factors to prevent recurrence.

Techniques Used:
Manual Therapy Strength Building Flexibility Training Soft Tissue Release Neuromuscular Re-education
3
Weeks 4–8
Rehabilitation

Sport-specific training is introduced as you regain strength and confidence. Proprioception and balance exercises challenge neuromuscular control, while functional movement patterns prepare you for the demands of your sport. Progressive load management ensures tissue adaptation without overloading.

Techniques Used:
Sport-Specific Training Proprioception Balance Training Functional Movement Sports Taping
4
Weeks 8–12
Return to Sport

The final phase involves validated performance testing to objectively confirm you're ready for full competition. A gradual return-to-training program ensures confidence and minimises re-injury risk. We provide an ongoing maintenance plan and education for long-term athletic health.

Techniques Used:
Performance Testing Gradual Return Protocol Movement Screening Injury Prevention Plan Education & Self-care

Our Approach

Techniques & Modalities

We combine evidence-based techniques tailored to your specific injury and recovery goals.

Manual Therapy

Manual therapy is a cornerstone of sports injury rehabilitation at PhysioElite. Our therapists use highly skilled hands-on techniques to restore joint mobility, reduce pain, and improve tissue extensibility. Manual therapy accelerates recovery by directly addressing musculoskeletal dysfunction and enhancing neuromuscular control.

  • Joint Mobilisation — Graded passive movements to restore normal joint mechanics and reduce pain
  • Soft Tissue Therapy — Targeted massage and myofascial release to reduce muscle tension and improve circulation
  • Trigger Point Release — Precise pressure techniques to deactivate painful muscle knots
  • Joint Manipulation — High-velocity, low-amplitude thrust techniques to restore joint mobility
Exercise Rehabilitation

Exercise is medicine. Our evidence-based exercise rehabilitation programs are individually designed to rebuild strength, flexibility, and endurance following injury. Progressively loaded therapeutic exercises promote tissue healing, improve neuromuscular control, and develop the physical capacity needed for safe return to sport.

  • Therapeutic Exercise Programs — Individually prescribed exercise protocols aligned with your recovery phase
  • Progressive Strength Training — Targeted resistance work to rebuild muscle strength around injured structures
  • Flexibility & Mobility — Stretching and movement drills to restore full range of motion
  • Sport-Specific Conditioning — Movement patterns and energy system training tailored to your sport
Sports Taping

Sports taping is a versatile tool used to support injured structures, modify movement patterns, and facilitate neuromuscular feedback. Our therapists are trained in multiple taping methodologies and select the most appropriate technique based on your injury type, activity level, and treatment goals.

  • Kinesio Taping — Elastic therapeutic tape that supports tissues while allowing full range of movement
  • Rigid Strapping — Firm support taping for acute injuries and high-demand sporting activities
  • McConnell Taping — Patellar correction taping for patellofemoral pain syndrome
  • Deload Taping — Offloading techniques for tendinopathy and overuse conditions
Dry Needling

Dry needling is an advanced technique using fine acupuncture needles inserted directly into myofascial trigger points — tight bands within muscles that cause local and referred pain. The technique stimulates a local twitch response, releasing the trigger point and restoring normal muscle function. It's particularly effective for chronic muscle pain and sports-related tension.

  • Myofascial Trigger Point Treatment — Directly targeting active and latent trigger points to relieve pain
  • Intramuscular Stimulation — Needle insertion into deep muscle layers to release chronic tension
  • Periosteal Pecking — Bone-level stimulation for tendinopathy and bone-related pain
  • Combined Approach — Dry needling integrated with exercise and manual therapy for optimal outcomes
Electrotherapy

Electrotherapy encompasses a range of modalities that use electrical or acoustic energy to accelerate tissue healing, reduce pain and inflammation, and improve muscle function. These adjunctive therapies complement hands-on treatment and exercise rehabilitation for comprehensive, faster recovery outcomes.

  • TENS (Transcutaneous Electrical Nerve Stimulation) — Pain relief through electrical nerve stimulation and endorphin release
  • Therapeutic Ultrasound — Deep tissue heating and cellular stimulation to accelerate healing
  • Low-Level Laser Therapy (LLLT) — Photobiomodulation to reduce inflammation and promote tissue repair
  • Interferential Therapy (IFT) — Medium-frequency currents for deeper pain relief and muscle stimulation
