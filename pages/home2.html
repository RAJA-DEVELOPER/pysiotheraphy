Our Approach

Treatment Modalities

A comprehensive toolkit of evidence-based therapies, tailored to your specific condition and recovery goals.

Manual Therapy

Hands-on physiotherapy techniques applied directly to joints, muscles, and soft tissues. Our skilled therapists use precise manual techniques to restore mobility, reduce pain, and improve function — often delivering immediate relief.

  • Joint Mobilisation & Manipulation — restoring joint range of motion
  • Soft Tissue Massage — releasing muscle tension and adhesions
  • Myofascial Release — targeting fascial restrictions and trigger points
  • Neural Mobilisation — improving nerve mobility and reducing neuropathic pain

Exercise Therapy

Individualised therapeutic exercise programmes designed to rebuild strength, improve flexibility, and restore normal movement patterns. Each programme is progressively adjusted based on your recovery milestones.

  • Strength & Resistance Training — rebuilding muscle after injury or surgery
  • Neuromuscular Re-education — retraining movement patterns and coordination
  • Proprioception & Balance Training — reducing fall risk and re-injury
  • Functional Movement Screening — identifying and correcting dysfunctional patterns

Electrotherapy

Advanced electrical stimulation modalities to accelerate tissue healing, manage pain, and reduce inflammation. These non-invasive treatments complement hands-on therapy to maximise your recovery.

  • TENS (Transcutaneous Electrical Nerve Stimulation) — effective pain relief
  • Ultrasound Therapy — promoting deep tissue healing and reducing scar tissue
  • Interferential Current (IFC) — targeting deep muscle and joint pain
  • LASER Therapy — reducing inflammation and accelerating cellular repair

Hydrotherapy

Water-based rehabilitation that harnesses buoyancy and resistance to facilitate movement and exercise in a low-impact environment. Ideal for post-surgical recovery and conditions where land-based exercise is too painful.

  • Aquatic Exercise Programme — joint-friendly strengthening and conditioning
  • Warm Water Immersion — relaxing muscles and improving circulation
  • Resistance Hydrotherapy — building strength using water resistance
  • Balance & Gait Retraining — safe environment for restoring normal walking

Dry Needling

A precise technique using fine monofilament needles to target myofascial trigger points, releasing muscle tension, reducing pain, and restoring normal muscle function — without the use of any medication.

  • Trigger Point Dry Needling — deactivating painful muscle knots
  • Deep Tissue Needling — targeting deeper layers of muscle and fascia
  • Peri-neural Dry Needling — addressing nerve-related referred pain
  • Electrical Dry Needling (E-stim) — combining needles with gentle electrical impulse

Sports Taping

Professional taping techniques to support injured structures, facilitate healing, and allow athletes to continue training safely. We use both rigid and elastic tape systems based on your individual needs.

  • Kinesiology Taping (KT Tape) — supporting muscles and reducing pain without restricting movement
  • Rigid Strapping — providing maximum joint stability for acute injuries
  • McConnell Taping — correcting patellofemoral alignment and knee tracking
  • Lymphatic Taping — reducing swelling and improving circulation post-injury
