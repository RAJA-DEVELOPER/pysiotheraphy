Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your first visit.

You can book your first appointment online using our booking widget below, by calling us on +61 3 9876 5432, or by filling in the contact form on this page. We also welcome walk-in enquiries during clinic hours. Online bookings receive instant confirmation via email and SMS.

Please bring a valid photo ID, your Medicare card or private health insurance card, any referral letters from your GP or specialist, previous imaging results (X-rays, MRI scans), and comfortable, loose-fitting clothing appropriate for the area being treated. Arrive 10 minutes early to complete your intake paperwork.

Your initial consultation is typically 60 minutes. This includes a comprehensive assessment of your condition, medical history review, movement analysis, and development of a personalised treatment plan. Follow-up sessions are usually 30–45 minutes depending on your treatment needs.

We do accept walk-ins, subject to practitioner availability. However, we strongly recommend booking in advance to secure your preferred time and therapist. For urgent injuries or acute pain, please call us and we will do our best to accommodate you on the same day.

We are accredited with all major Australian private health funds including Medibank, Bupa, HCF, nib, AHM, CBHS, HBF, and many others. We use HICAPS for on-the-spot rebate claims — simply swipe your card after your appointment. We also accept WorkCover, TAC, DVA, and Medicare EPC referrals.

Our fees are:
  • Initial Consultation (60 min): $160
  • Standard Follow-up (30 min): $95
  • Extended Follow-up (45 min): $125
  • Telehealth Consultation: $85

With private health insurance, your out-of-pocket cost is typically $20–$60 per session depending on your level of cover. Medicare EPC rebates apply with a valid GP referral (5 sessions per year).

Yes. We ask for 24 hours' notice for cancellations or rescheduling. You can cancel online through the patient portal, by calling us, or by emailing hello@physioelite.com.au. Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice may incur a $40 late-cancellation fee. We understand that emergencies happen — please contact us as soon as possible.

We offer secure video telehealth appointments for follow-up consultations, exercise prescription reviews, and clinical advice — ideal for patients who live remotely, have mobility limitations, or prefer the convenience of home. Telehealth sessions are conducted via our secure patient portal. Medicare EPC and most private health funds provide rebates for telehealth physiotherapy.
