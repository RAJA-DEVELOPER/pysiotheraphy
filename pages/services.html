Rebate Information

Many of our services are eligible for private health insurance rebates. The amount you can claim depends on your level of cover and your health fund. We recommend contacting your insurer prior to your first appointment.

  • Medicare: Rebates available through GP Management Plans (GPMP) and Team Care Arrangements (TCA).
  • DVA: We're a registered DVA provider — no out-of-pocket costs for eligible veterans.
  • Private Health: Extras cover typically includes physiotherapy at varying benefit levels.
  • WorkCover & CTP: Accepted for work-related and motor vehicle injury claims.
  • NDIS: Services available for plan-managed and self-managed participants.

How to Claim Online

  • 1
    Confirm Your Cover

    Log in to your health fund's app or website to check your physiotherapy extras benefit.

  • 2
    Bring Your Card

    Bring your health fund membership card or digital card to your appointment.

  • 3
    Instant HICAPS Claim

    We swipe your card via our HICAPS terminal — your rebate is applied instantly at the checkout.

  • 4
    Pay the Gap Only

    You only pay the remaining gap after the rebate. We'll email you a full receipt.
