Our Facilities

World-Class Clinical Environments

Designed with patient comfort and clinical effectiveness in mind, every corner of PhysioElite supports your recovery journey.

Treatment Rooms

State-of-the-Art Treatment Rooms

Our 12 private treatment rooms are purpose-built to deliver a calm, clinical and comfortable experience. Each room is equipped with the latest physiotherapy technology and ergonomic furnishings.

  • Fully soundproofed private consultation rooms
  • Height-adjustable electric treatment tables
  • Advanced electrotherapy and ultrasound units
  • Climate-controlled for patient comfort
  • Digital record systems for real-time progress tracking
Rehab Equipment

Advanced Rehabilitation Equipment

Our 2,000 sq ft rehabilitation gym features cutting-edge equipment supporting the full spectrum of recovery — from gentle mobility work to high-performance strength training.

  • Hydrotherapy pool and warm-water therapy bays
  • Anti-gravity treadmill for low-impact gait retraining
  • Isokinetic dynamometry for objective strength assessment
  • Motion-capture analysis system
  • Pilates reformers and functional movement rigs
