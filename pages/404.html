While You're Here, Here's a Quick Health Tip

TIP 1 / 3
10-Minute Morning Stretch Routine for Back Pain

Starting your day with gentle stretches can significantly reduce chronic back pain. Focus on hip flexors, hamstrings, and spinal rotation for maximum benefit. Consistency is key — just 10 minutes daily makes a world of difference.

TIP 2 / 3
When to See a Physio vs. Your GP

If you're experiencing joint pain, muscle weakness, or difficulty moving after an injury — a physiotherapist is often your best first step. GPs are great for diagnosis; physios specialise in restoring function and movement.

TIP 3 / 3
5 Exercises for Better Posture at Work

Desk workers: try chin tucks, shoulder blade squeezes, and seated cat-cow stretches every hour. Setting a posture alarm every 30 minutes keeps your spine happy and reduces fatigue by up to 40% over your workday.
